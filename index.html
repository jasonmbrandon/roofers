Questions people ask us.

Will insurance pay for my roof?

Many policies cover sudden damage from wind, hail or a fallen tree, but not wear from age. We inspect for free, photograph the damage, and can meet your adjuster on the roof. Your policy and adjuster make the final call.

Repair or replace: how do I know?

If the damage is in one area and the roof has years left, we repair it. If it is near the end of its life or leaking in several places, a repair is money spent twice. We show you the photos and tell you which one we would choose for our own home.

How long does a new roof take?

Most shingle roofs are done in one or two days. Metal and tile take longer, and we give you the schedule in your written quote.

Do I need to be home?

Only for the start and the final walkthrough. We need access to the driveway and an outdoor outlet.

Do you offer financing?

Ask during your inspection and we will walk you through the payment options available for your job.

What warranty do I get?

Each roof comes with the material manufacturer's warranty plus our own workmanship warranty. The exact terms for your roof are written in your quote.
